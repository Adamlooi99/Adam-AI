🇲🇳 Mongolia — Gold & Copper Boom · XAUUSD · XAGUSD
MN-01 — Campaign Brief

The News & Instruments Affected

Mongolia's economic story is now a precious metals story — gold +86% YoY, copper +57% YoY, coal collapsing. All EBC instruments listed.

The News Event
Mongolia's copper exports surged 76% to $5.83B, overtaking coal as the nation's #1 earner for the first time. Gold prices jumped 86% year-on-year, boosting Mongolia's trade surplus and reserves. Meanwhile, coal revenues collapsed 34% despite record volumes — China's softening demand is crushing prices even as Mongolia ships more.

Rio Tinto's Oyu Tolgoi copper mine — set to be the world's 4th largest by 2030 — had exports temporarily blocked by protesters, exposing political risk. Mongolia's parliament launched an inquiry into the nation's financial arrangements with Rio Tinto. Economy projected to grow 5–5.6% in 2026, entirely dependent on commodity prices.
EBC Instruments in Focus
XAUUSD
Gold vs US Dollar
Mongolia's entire trade surplus, MNT strength, and GDP growth track gold price. +86% YoY pricing drives record export revenues.
XAGUSD
Silver vs US Dollar
Silver follows gold sentiment. Industrial demand from Mongolia's copper/mining narrative amplifies the precious metals trade.
Trading Context
Mongolia is landlocked and 91% export-dependent on China. Any China slowdown = MNT weakness + commodity pressure. But the gold/copper boom is a structural tailwind for XAUUSD as long as AI infrastructure and green energy demand persists. Oyu Tolgoi protests = copper supply-risk premium.

🇿🇦 South Africa — Gold Rally + Trade War Safe Haven · XAUUSD · XAGUSD · USDZAR
ZA-01 — Campaign Brief

The News & Instruments Affected

South Africa's JSE hit record highs on gold's relentless rally — while US tariffs and a SARB rate hike create a high-tension ZAR trade.

The News Event
The JSE All Share Index is near record highs — up 43.6% in 12 months — powered almost entirely by South Africa's gold and platinum miners. Impala Platinum surged +5%, Sibanye Stillwater jumped +8% on PGM price strength.

The backdrop is complex: The US imposed 30% tariffs on South African exports as punishment for BRICS alignment, and AGOA expired in October 2025 — threatening a $1.05B trade surplus collapse. But gold, platinum and critical minerals are explicitly tariff-exempt, making mining stocks a safe haven within a tariff-hit economy.

The SARB hiked rates in May for the first time in 3 years to defend against oil shock. USD/ZAR near 16.40 — ZAR at its strongest since mid-June on gold price support. Governor Kganyago has warned further hikes remain possible.
EBC Instruments in Focus
XAUUSD
Gold vs US Dollar
JSE and ZAR move in lockstep with gold. South Africa holds world's largest gold reserves. Gold rally = JSE rally = ZAR support.
XAGUSD
Silver vs US Dollar
PGM/precious metals sentiment play. South Africa's platinum and palladium dominance makes the whole PGM complex reactive to SA news.
USDZAR
USD vs South African Rand
Three-way tension: gold price support (ZAR bullish) vs US tariffs + BRICS risk (ZAR bearish) vs SARB rate hikes (ZAR supportive). High-volatility pair.
Context
Why 3 Instruments?
SA is unique: it lets you trade the same macro story three ways — commodities (XAUUSD), industrial metals sentiment (XAGUSD), and the currency reaction itself (USDZAR). Rare convergence of all three in one news cycle.
